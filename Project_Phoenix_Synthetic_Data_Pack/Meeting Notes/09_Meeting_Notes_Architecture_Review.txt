Purpose: root-cause the latency issue found in Week 11 load testing and align on data quality remediation plan.

1. Latency root cause (Jordan / Sarah)
Load test results: p95 response time 6.2s against a 3.5s target, consistent across three test runs. Sarah walked through the current RAG pipeline - customer message goes to Lex for coarse intent, then for anything outside the top-8 trained intents, the full conversation context plus a knowledge-base retrieval chunk gets sent to Bedrock. The retrieved context chunk has grown over the build (currently averaging ~2,800 tokens) as more FAQ content was added without pruning. Group agreed this is the primary suspect, alongside cold-start latency on the orchestrator Lambda when traffic is bursty.

Agreed actions:
- Jordan + Sarah: trim retrieval to top-3 most relevant chunks + summarise long chunks before adding to context (PHX-119)
- Jordan: move orchestrator Lambda to provisioned concurrency for the demo window to eliminate cold starts (PHX-118)
- Liam: confirmed DynamoDB read latency is not a material contributor (<200ms of the 6.2s), ruled out as root cause
- Re-test load scenario by end of day Monday 4 Aug, results to Aisha + Maria before Tue standup

Sarah left the call after this item to get back onto demo-prep work with Aisha.

2. Data quality findings (Fatima)
Fatima presented the reconciliation results: approximately 8% of customerId values in the legacy order-history extract are duplicated, traced to a 2021 merge of two historical loyalty programmes that assigned new IDs without deprecating the old ones. This wasn't visible in the Week 8 sample data (which happened to be drawn from post-merge records only) - it only surfaced once the full 3-year extract was reconciled in Week 10-11, which is why the Week 8 status report shows Data & Integration as Green and the Week 11 report shows Amber.

Agreed actions:
- Fatima: build a dedup/merge rule keyed on email + original loyalty number, target complete by 8 Aug (PHX-104)
- Priya: confirm with client data owner whether historical order value should be summed or take-latest on merge
- Group agreed this does not block the CTO demo (demo uses a clean curated dataset) but is on the critical path for UAT start
